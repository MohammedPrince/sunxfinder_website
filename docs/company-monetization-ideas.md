# SunXFinder: How Companies Can Pay

## The short answer

Keep joining free. Charge companies for three things:

1. **More customers:** paid access to more quote requests.
2. **More visibility:** ads inside the app and on the website.
3. **Direct reach:** push notifications to customers beyond their own.

Recommended main model: **a free plan with limits, plus monthly paid plans, plus paid ads on top.**

A company will pay when it can see that SunXFinder brought it customers. The first job is to prove that with numbers. See [What to show companies](#what-to-show-companies).

Prices in this document are starting guesses, not market research. Test them with real companies and convert them to SDG when you quote.

| Idea | What the company gets | How to charge | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **App home slider** | Its banner on the app home screen | Per week or month. Sliders already have start and end dates | Ready now |

**Improve these first (small build):**

- **Slider clicks and views:** sliders have no click count today, so you cannot yet prove results. Without proof, companies will not renew.
- **Limits on "open all customers":** today it is an on/off switch. Sell it as a set number of sends per month, so customers do not get flooded.
- **Slider slots:** sell position 1, 2 and 3 at different prices.
- **Sponsored label:** mark every paid placement as "Sponsored" (`ممول`).

## 2. Paid access to customers

This is the strongest income, because quote requests are the real value of SunXFinder.

| Idea | How it works | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Offer limit on the free plan** | Free companies send a set number of offers per month. Paid plans send more or unlimited | Small build |
| **Offer credits** | Companies buy packs of credits, and each offer uses one. Good for small companies that do not want a monthly fee | Small build |
| **Early access** | Paid companies see new open requests first, for example 2 to 6 hours before free companies | Small build |
| **Priority alerts** | Instant push or email for new requests, filtered by city and system size | Small build |
| **Boosted offer** | The company pays to show its offer at the top of the customer's offer list, labelled "Sponsored" | Small build |
| **City sponsor** | One company becomes the sponsor of a city for a month, shown first to customers in that city | Small build |
| **Sponsored company list spot** | Paid spots above the normal company list, kept separate from the real rating order | Small build |
| **Recommended after the calculator** | After the calculator, the app shows sponsored companies next to the suggested system | Small build |
| **Direct request button** | Only paid companies get "Request a quote from this company" on their profile | Small build |
| **Commission on won projects** | A percentage of each accepted project | Bigger build, not recommended first |

**About commission:** it looks attractive because you earn more on big projects, but customers pay companies outside your system. It is hard to collect and easy to avoid. Try it only later, or as a small fixed fee when an offer is accepted. Your invoices and accepted offers already give you the record to calculate it.

## 6. Others who can pay

Do not rely only on solar companies.

| Who | What they buy |
| --- | --- |
| **Suppliers and brands** (panels, inverters, batteries) | "Recommended brand" in the calculator, brand page, slider ads, sponsored product lists |
| **Banks and finance companies** | Installment offers beside the quote, paid per lead |
| **Insurance companies** | Solar system cover offered after handover |
| **Electricians and technicians** | Paid listing for small jobs and repairs |
| **Generator and battery sellers** | Banner ads |
| **Website visitors** | Website banners and sponsored guide articles |

Suppliers are worth approaching early: they often have bigger marketing budgets than installers, and you already have a suppliers list.

## 8. Suggested order

**Step 1: Prove value (now, stay free)**

- Get companies and customers active.
- Add slider click tracking.
- Record the numbers per company: requests seen, offers sent, offers won, project value.

**Step 2: Sell ads by hand**

- Offer sliders, carousel and campaign pushes to your 5 to 10 best companies.
- Take payment by bank transfer and switch the ad on from the admin panel.
- This needs almost no new code and shows who will really pay.

**Step 3: Announce the plans**

- Give 30 to 60 days' notice.
- Offer founding companies a discount for life.
- Do not remove free features without warning.

**Step 4: Build plans and limits**

- Plans, offer limits, early access and an admin screen to activate payments.

**Step 5: Add other payers**

- Suppliers, finance partners and premium extras.

Do not start charging for offers while customer requests are few. If companies pay and receive no requests, they leave and tell others.

## 9. What not to do

These protect the trust that makes the platform worth paying for.

- **Do not sell ratings.** Never let payment change stars, or hide bad reviews.
- **Do not sell the "Verified" badge.** It must always be earned.
- **Do not hide paid placements.** Always label them "Sponsored".
- **Do not flood customers.** Set a limit, for example no more than 1 or 2 paid pushes per customer each week.
- **Do not share customer contact details** with companies they never dealt with. "Open all customers" should send the push through SunXFinder, as it does today.
- **Do not let customers pay to fix a problem.** Requests and comparing offers stay free for customers.

**Before selling pushes:** update Terms and Privacy to say customers may receive sponsored messages, and let them turn these off in the app. Both pages can be edited from Admin → System → Privacy & Terms. Confirm the wording with a lawyer.

## 10. Collecting payment

Start simple:

1. Send the company an invoice from SunXFinder.
2. The company pays by bank transfer or mobile banking and sends the receipt.
3. Admin records the payment and activates the plan until an end date.
4. The system warns the company before expiry, then moves it back to Free.

An online payment gateway can come later. Check first which ones work with Sudanese banks and accounts. Decide whether you price in USD or SDG, and for how long a quoted SDG price stays valid.

## What to show companies

Send each company a monthly report like this:

> **Example Solar Company, October** (sample numbers)
> 40 new requests in your area · 12 offers sent · 3 won · SDG 90,000,000 in project value
> Your slider: 8,200 views · 310 clicks
> You missed 28 requests because you reached your offer limit.

The last line is what makes a free company upgrade.

**Sales message:**

> "Joining stays free. Last month, 40 customers in Khartoum asked for solar on SunXFinder. Silver and Gold companies see those requests first and can send more offers. One extra project pays for the whole year."

**Track these numbers:**

| Number | Why |
| --- | --- |
| New requests per month, per city | Shows the value you are selling |
| Offers per request | Above 3 or so, companies compete and will pay for priority |
| Offers won | Proof for customers and companies |
| Slider views and clicks | Proof for advertisers |
| Companies that renew | Shows whether paid features really work |
| Pushes turned off | Warns you that customers are getting too many |

## Decisions for you

1. Which plan is the main offer: monthly plans, offer credits, or both?
2. Price in USD or SDG?
3. How many free offers per month, once you have enough requests?
4. Should existing companies get a founding discount?
5. Are you willing to approach suppliers before companies?
