# SunXFinder: Launch and Marketing Plan

## The short answer

| Question | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| **Publish the Facebook page now or later?** | **Now.** It costs nothing, takes months to grow, and gives you credibility when you call companies. Post useful solar content; you do not need companies listed to do that. |
| **Publish the app now or later?** | **After you have enough companies.** Aim for at least 20 approved companies with complete profiles in your first one or two cities. A customer who opens an empty app deletes it and does not come back. |
| **Get customers first, to show companies?** | **No, get companies first, but collect proof of demand before the app is public.** Use the Facebook page, the website calculator and WhatsApp to collect real people who want solar. Show those numbers to companies. |
| **But customers need companies in the app?** | Yes, so **you fill the app with companies yourself.** Joining is free, you build their profiles for them, and you send them the first leads by hand. |

**Rule:** companies first, customers second, money third.

Never invent customer numbers to convince companies. One company that finds out tells all the others.

---

## Why companies come first

SunXFinder is a marketplace: customers come for companies, companies stay for customers. You must start one side, and companies are the easier one:

- **There are few of them.** You need about 20 to 50, not thousands of customers.
- **You can reach them one by one.** Most solar companies in Sudan already have a Facebook page and a WhatsApp number.
- **Joining is free** and costs them nothing but a few minutes.
- **An empty app kills customer trust**, while an empty customer list does not upset a company that paid nothing.

Customers are cheaper to get once the app has something to show.

## Phase 0: Prepare (weeks 1 to 2, now)

**Goal:** everything ready before anyone sees it.

| Task | Details |
| --- | --- |
| **Create the Facebook page now** | Arabic first, English second. Name, logo, cover, "Find. Compare. Connect." Link to the website. |
| **Also create** | A WhatsApp Business number, an Instagram account, a TikTok account. Same name and logo everywhere. |
| **Google Play account** | If it is a new personal developer account, Google asks for a closed test with at least 12 testers for 14 days before production. Start this early. |
| **Fix the backend gaps** | Ratings, delete account, quote complete and deep links are listed as "not live" in the app overview. Store reviewers and early customers will hit them. |
| **Store listing** | Arabic and English text, 6 to 8 screenshots in Arabic, a short video. |
| **Terms and Privacy** | Mention sponsored messages and the opt-out before any campaign push is sold. |
| **Prepare a company pitch** | One page in Arabic (PDF or image) that explains what SunXFinder does for a company. See [Company pitch](#company-pitch). |

## Phase 1: Get companies (weeks 2 to 8)

**Goal:** 20 or more approved companies with complete profiles in one or two launch cities.

### Where to find them

- Facebook search: "طاقة شمسية" + city name, and solar groups.
- Shops that sell panels, batteries and inverters (محولات).
- Suppliers and importers: they know every installer and may introduce you.
- Electricians and installers who post their work on Facebook and TikTok.

### How to sign them up

1. **Call or message the owner on WhatsApp.** Keep it short: "We list solar companies for free. Customers send you quote requests. Can we create your page?"
2. **Build the profile for them.** Take their logo, photos, services and products from their Facebook page, with their permission. Most will not do it themselves.
3. **Show them their page** on the website. A ready page convinces better than a pitch.
4. **Teach one person in the company** to reply to quote requests from the company panel. Five minutes on a call.

### The "founding company" offer

Give the first 30 companies something that costs you little:

- A **"Founding company"** badge on the profile (a badge, not "Verified": Verified must stay earned).
- **A discount for life** on paid plans when they start.
- **Free home slider week** after the app launches.
- **Free campaign push** to announce their offer once customers arrive.

### What to track

| Number | Target at the end of phase 1 |
| --- | --- |
| Companies approved | 20+ |
| Profiles complete (logo, about, products, phone) | 90% |
| Companies that tested a quote reply | 10+ |
| Cities covered | 1 or 2 |

## Phase 1, in parallel: Collect demand without the app

**Goal:** prove to companies that people want solar, using real numbers.

You do not need the app for this. You need the Facebook page and the website.

| What | How |
| --- | --- |
| **Useful posts, 3 to 4 a week** | "How many panels for a fridge and 3 fans?", "Lithium or gel battery?", "How to spot fake panels", "What does a 5 kW system run?". Short videos work best. |
| **"Free solar calculation" posts** | People comment or message what they want to run; you answer with the website calculator result and the link. |
| **Website calculator** | Every calculation is a sign of demand. Count them. |
| **Hand-made leads (concierge)** | When someone wants a price, take their details with permission and enter the request in the system for them, or send it to two or three listed companies. This is the first value you give companies. |
| **Join solar and city groups** | Answer questions honestly. Do not spam links. |
| **Small Facebook ads** | Once you have 10 good posts, boost the best ones in your launch city. Start with $3 to $5 a day. |

Then you can tell a company the truth:

> "Last month, 140 people in Khartoum asked us about solar, and we sent you 6 of them. When the app opens next month, they will send requests to you directly."

## Phase 2: Soft launch the app (weeks 8 to 12)

**Goal:** real customers send real requests, and companies reply.

Publish the app when all of these are true:

- [ ] 20+ approved companies with complete profiles in the launch city
- [ ] At least 10 companies have replied to a test request
- [ ] The full flow works: request → offer → accept → progress → complete → rating
- [ ] The closed test passed and Google approved the listing
- [ ] Facebook page has some followers and steady posts

**How to launch:**

1. Launch in **one or two cities first**, not the whole country. Show only cities where companies exist.
2. Send the Play Store link to everyone who messaged you in phase 1. They are your first customers.
3. Ask every listed company to share the app with **their own customers and Facebook followers**, using their deep link (`/companies/slug/...`). The link opens their own profile in the app. This is the cheapest customer source you have.
4. Post the launch on Facebook with a short video of the calculator and a quote request.
5. Check every new request by hand for the first weeks. Call companies that do not reply within a day.

**The most important rule in this phase:** every customer request must get at least one offer, fast. If customers get no replies, the app dies.

| Number | Target |
| --- | --- |
| Installs | 500 to 1,000 |
| Quote requests per week | 20+ |
| Requests with at least one offer | 90% |
| Average offers per request | 2 or more |
| Time to first offer | Under 24 hours |

## Phase 3: Grow (month 4 onward)

**Goal:** more customers, more cities, first income.

### Customers

| Channel | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Facebook and Instagram ads** | Target the launch cities. Advertise the calculator ("Find out what system you need, free"), not the app name. |
| **TikTok and Reels** | Short videos: a real installation, a customer's story, "what 1 kW runs". |
| **Company sharing** | Give companies a ready image and text in Arabic to share. Each company has followers you do not. |
| **Calculator result sharing** | The app can already share the result as an image. Ask customers to send it to family before deciding. |
| **Seasonal peaks** | Hot season, power outages, Ramadan. Raise ad spend at these times. |
| **Reviews** | After each finished project, ask the customer to rate the company, and ask happy customers to rate the app on Google Play. |
| **Referral** | Later: reward customers who bring friends (not built yet). |

### Companies

- Add a new city only when you have about 10 companies there.
- Approach suppliers and brands (panels, batteries, inverters): they have bigger marketing budgets than installers.
- Send each company a monthly report: requests in their area, offers sent, offers won.

### Income

Follow [company-monetization-ideas.md](company-monetization-ideas.md). In short:

1. **Sell ads by hand first:** home slider, carousel and campaign pushes, paid by bank transfer.
2. **Announce paid plans** only when companies get regular requests, with 30 to 60 days' notice.
3. **Do not charge for offers while requests are few.** A company that pays and gets nothing leaves and tells others.

## Content plan for the Facebook page

Post 3 to 4 times a week. Arabic first; add English in the same post or a second post.

| Day | Type | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sunday | Education | "How many panels do you need for a fridge, a TV and 4 fans?" |
| Tuesday | Calculation | "Send us what you want to run, and we will calculate your system for free." |
| Thursday | Trust | "How to check that a panel is original", "Questions to ask before you pay a company" |
| Saturday | Company or project | A listed company's finished project, with its permission and tag. |

**Do not** post only "Download our app". People follow pages that help them, not pages that sell to them.

## Company pitch

Use this message, in Arabic, when you contact a company:

> "SunXFinder is a free platform where customers find solar companies, calculate the system they need and ask for prices. We create your company page for free, and customers send quote requests directly to you. You reply from your company panel. There is no cost to join."

When you have demand numbers, add one line:

> "Last month, [real number] people in [city] asked us about solar."

| Facebook and Instagram ads, after launch | $150 to $300 |
| Phone and internet for calls | small |
| Designer for posts (optional) | $50 to $100 |
| Google Play developer account | $25 once |

Spend most of your time, not money, in phase 1. Calls to companies are worth more than ads at this stage.

---

## Rules from the app to keep

- Every paid placement carries the **Sponsored** (`ممول`) label (`is_sponsored: true`).
- Respect the customer's setting to turn sponsored pushes off. Service pushes (quotes, offers, projects) are not affected.
- Send pushes in the customer's language.
- No more than 1 or 2 sponsored pushes per customer per week.
- Never sell ratings or the Verified badge.
- Never give customer contact details to companies they did not contact.
