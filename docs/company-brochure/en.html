The platform

What is SunXFinder?

SunXFinder is a marketplace that connects solar companies with customers who need a solar system. Customers add their appliances to our load calculator, see the panels, inverter and batteries they need, then ask companies for prices.

You receive the request, send your offer and manage the whole project from your company panel. No chasing leads, and no guessing what the customer needs.

Every request comes with the numbers

  • Daily energy use
  • Peak load
  • Suggested panels, inverter and batteries

How a customer becomes your project

  1. 1

    Customer asks

    A customer calculates their load and sends a quote request.

  2. 2

    You send an offer

    One total price, with an invoice and a message.

  3. 3

    Customer chooses

    They compare offers and accept the one they like.

  4. 4

    You install

    A project is created. You post progress until handover.

  5. 5

    Customer rates you

    Good ratings bring you more customers.
