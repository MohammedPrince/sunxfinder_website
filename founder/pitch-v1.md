**SunXFinder** (sunxfinder.com, Android app, Arabic and English)

- Enter the appliances you want to run and the hours you need at night; the free calculator gives a rough system size (panels, batteries, inverter).
- Send one quote request; solar companies listed in your city (Khartoum or Port Sudan) reply with written offers listing brands, models, warranty and price, so you can compare them side by side.
- Your phone number only goes to the companies you choose to deal with.
- After the job, you rate the company, and you can see other customers' ratings of finished projects.
- It is free for customers. Companies join free too; later they may pay for ads, which are always labelled "Sponsored".
- Launch week: send us any solar quote you already have and we will check it for free (sizes, brands, warranty, protection).

"Buying" here means: you would use SunXFinder to send a quote request within the next month. The four price questions are about the quote check: after launch week it may become a paid service. Give those prices in US dollars for one check.
