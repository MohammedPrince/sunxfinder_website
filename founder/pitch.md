**SunXFinder Checked Quotes** (sunxfinder.com, Android app, Arabic and English)

- Enter the appliances you want to run; the free calculator gives a rough system size (panels, batteries, inverter).
- Send one request. Solar companies in Khartoum or Port Sudan reply with written offers listing brand, model, warranty and price. If fewer than 2 companies reply within 48 hours, we phone companies in your city for you and tell you what we found.
- Already have a quote or an installer you trust? Send us the quote and we check it for free, or add your installer and we invite them to quote next to the others.
- Companies see only your name until you accept their offer. If a company you did not choose calls you, tell us and we remove it from SunXFinder.
- Before you confirm the handover, the company sends photos of the installed panel, battery and inverter labels so you can match them to the offer, even from abroad. If something is wrong, tap "Report a problem" and the job goes back to the company. Ratings come only from customers who confirmed a finished job.
- Free for customers. In launch week we check the first 50 quotes free by hand; after that the check may cost a small fee.

"Buying" here means: you would use SunXFinder to send a quote request within the next month. The four price questions are about the quote check: after launch week it may become a paid service. Give those prices in US dollars for one check.
