# Buyer panel: SunXFinder: a free website and Android app in Sudan to size a home solar system and compare written offers from several local solar companies

**7 buy · 13 pass** (35% buy) out of 20 simulated buyers. Seed 315520, so the same cards can be dealt again.

These are simulated buyers, not customers. Use this to find objections and weak spots, then confirm the big ones with real people before you spend.

## Why they pass

| reason | buyers | in their words |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| trust | 7 | "When I need solar I call the installer my nephew used, someone I can see and complain to at his door. A website with strangers' ratings means less to me than what my neighbours say, and I don't want my number going to companies I don't know." (P004) · "I buy my parts in the market where I can hold the battery and argue the price, and my electrician wires it for me. An app sending me offers from companies I don't know doesn't tell me whether the panel is real or fake, so I'd rather walk the shops myself." (P005) |
| habit | 3 | "When I buy solar I will ask my brother-in-law and the neighbours which installer they used, not send my request to companies I don't know through a phone app. And with the dollar moving every week, written offers from strangers will not hold until I am ready to pay." (P009) · "I buy my panels and batteries myself in the market, shop by shop, and the electrician my cousin used wires it for me. I don't need an app to send my request to companies I've never heard of. They'll just quote me shop prices plus their profit." (P011) |
| timing | 2 | "I already have an installer and I'm almost done deciding with the company I spoke to, so starting over with a new quote request now just delays getting power back in my shop. Maybe I'd send you the quote I have to check, but I won't ask other companies to start calling me." (P015) · "I already have an installer and I have spoken to a company; I am about to decide. I am not going to start over and wait for new offers from people I don't know through an app that just launched." (P020) |
| price | 1 | "I might play with the free calculator, but I don't have the money for a full system this month, so asking companies for quotes now just means phone calls I can't follow through on. When I'm ready I'll ask in the Khartoum solar group first anyway, people there tell you real prices." (P013) |

## What would flip a no

- If a relative or neighbour told me they got a good installer through it, or if the free check of my nephew's installer's quote found something real that saved me money.
- If a shop owner I know in my area told me they got a system through it and it held up through the power cuts, or if the companies had to stand behind the warranty with SunXFinder holding them to it.
- If my relative or a neighbour told me they got a good installer through SunXFinder and the price was better than the one we know, then I would send a request, maybe through WhatsApp.
- Someone in my city Facebook group saying they got real offers through it within a day or two, with the names of the companies that replied.
- If someone I trust in my family or street used it and told me the company it found did good work and kept its price.
- If someone I know in the market used it and told me the company it found held the price and did good work, or if my own installer's quote got checked and it found something wrong with it.
- If my relative's installer or a shop I already trust was on it, or someone I know said the quotes came in cheaper than the market, I might send one request just to compare.
- Seeing a real offer with a payment-in-instalments option, or a small starter system price I can actually afford, posted by someone in my Facebook group who used the app.
- Proof of the finished job sent to me before final payment, like photos or video of the installed panels, battery and inverter labels matched against the quote, or someone independent confirming it on site.
- If the free check of my current quote showed real problems with the sizes or brands, and I could get one or two better offers without giving anyone my number until I chose them.
- If people I know in my Facebook group said they got a cheaper, honest quote through SunXFinder, or if my relative's installer was listed on it so I could compare their price with the others.
- Seeing that the app prices are really lower than what I find in the market for the same brands, for example a neighbour who used it and paid less, or being able to get parts prices only and use my own electrician.

Buyers say they would buy **1.0 times** in the first month on average.
