# SunXFinder: the idea

**What:** a free solar marketplace for Sudan: a website (sunxfinder.com) and an Android app, in Arabic and English.

**For customers (free):** calculate the solar system their home or shop needs, find solar companies in their city, request quotes, compare written offers from several companies, follow the project, and rate the company.

**For companies (free to join now):** a company page, quote requests from customers, a panel to reply with offers and invoices, and reports. Later they pay for plans (more offers, early access to requests) and in-app ads (home slider, carousel, campaign pushes). See [docs/company-monetization-ideas.md](../docs/company-monetization-ideas.md).

**Where:** Sudan. Launch cities: Khartoum and/or Port Sudan.

**Order:** companies first, customers second, money third ([docs/marketing-plan.md](../docs/marketing-plan.md)).
