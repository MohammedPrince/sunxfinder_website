# Buyer panel: SunXFinder: a free website and Android app in Sudan to size a home solar system and compare written offers from several local solar companies

**8 buy · 12 pass** (40% buy) out of 20 simulated buyers. Seed 315520, so the same cards can be dealt again.

These are simulated buyers, not customers. Use this to find objections and weak spots, then confirm the big ones with real people before you spend.

## What would flip a no

- A relative or neighbour telling me they used it and the system they got matched what was promised.
- If a shop owner I know in my area told me they used it and the system held up through the cuts, or if the check could actually test that a battery or panel is genuine, not just read the sticker.
- A relative or neighbour telling me they used it and the price came out lower than what my cousin's installer quoted.
- Someone in my Facebook group posting that they got real quotes through it, with the company names and prices, and saying the installation went fine.
- If someone in my family or a neighbour used it and told me the company was honest and the price they wrote was the price they charged.
- A relative or a shop owner near me telling me they used it and the price they got held, or the check found a fake battery in their installer's quote.
- If my relative or a neighbour told me they got a cheaper price through it than in the market, I'd send our electrician's quote to get it checked for free.
- Seeing a small starter system (a few panels and one battery for lights, fans and a fridge) with a real price or monthly installments that comes in below what I spend on fuel each month, and people in my city's Facebook group saying they used SunXFinder and it was honest.
- If I could send the one quote I already have and get it checked the same day, without my number going to any company, I might do that before I sign.
- If people in my Facebook group posted that SunXFinder checked their quote and caught a fake battery or an overpriced inverter, I'd send them my cousin's installer's quote to check for free.
- Seeing that the offers come in at or below what I get haggling in the market, or a free check telling me whether the parts prices I was quoted at the shops are fair.
- Seeing that the quote check is done fast on the website without installing anything, by a real person who answers on WhatsApp, and that it found a real problem in someone else's quote I can see.

Buyers say they would buy **1.0 times** in the first month on average.
